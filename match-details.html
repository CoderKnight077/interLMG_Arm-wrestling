Match Rules

  • Both competitors must keep their free hand on the peg during the match.
  • Shoulders must remain square to the table and cannot cross the center line.
  • The match starts with the referee's "Ready to Go" command.
  • A pin is achieved when the wrist touches or goes below the touch pad.
  • Intentional slip results in a foul; two fouls result in match forfeit.
  • False starts will result in an immediate foul warning.
  • Back pressure is limited to 25% of body weight.
  • The referee's decision is final and binding.
